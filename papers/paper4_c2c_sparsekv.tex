\begin{abstract}
Long-context agentic LLMs increasingly exhaust GPU HBM capacity, forcing serving engines (Mooncake, HiCache, Dynamo) into 3-tier \texttt{HBM}$\leftrightarrow$\texttt{DRAM}$\leftrightarrow$\texttt{SSD} KV offloading. Recent production analyses (\texttt{FlatCake}) show DRAM staging pool exhaustion accounts for $41\%$ of TTFT, while zero-copy \emph{dense} C2C attention (\texttt{DirectKV}) hits the $210\text{ GB/s}$ Grace--Blackwell C2C bandwidth wall ($\approx 25\times$ slower than HBM3e).

We introduce \textbf{\texttt{C2C-SparseKV}}, a tierless KV serving architecture exploiting three previously uncharacterized Grace--Blackwell (\texttt{GB200}) hardware properties: (1) Blackwell 1D \texttt{TMA} (\texttt{cp.async.bulk}) executes \emph{natively on unpinned \texttt{mmap()} Hardware-ATS host virtual addresses} at full pinned bandwidth ($75.77$ vs.\ $75.73\text{ GB/s}$, exact $0.00\text{e+00}$ error) with zero ATS TLB cliff across $4\text{M}$-token pools even on 4\,KB pages; (2) Grace's LPDDR5X memory arbiter enforces strict hardware QoS priority for Blackwell C2C streams, suffering \textbf{$0.0\%$ GPU slowdown} even while $64$ Grace Neoverse-V2 cores hammer the same two LPDDR5X controllers at \textbf{$1.41\text{ TB/s}$}; and (3) under top-$K$ sparse attention ($K_{\text{sel}}{=}2\text{K}$), fusing Blackwell's native \texttt{cvt.rn.f16x2.e2m1x2} (\texttt{NVFP4} $1{\times}16$ KV, $3.20\times$ smaller $\to >12\text{M}$ tokens/tray) directly into 1D TMA-over-ATS shrinks per-layer C2C traffic to $0.65\text{ MB}$. Evaluated live on a full $4\times$\texttt{GB200} + $140$-core Dual-Grace tray:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Zero-Copy Multi-GPU Linear Scaling:} Across a live $96\text{ GiB}$ 48-layer LPDDR5X pool, 1D TMA-over-ATS scales $3.90\times$ across 4 GPUs ($25.49 \to 26.11\,\mu\text{s/layer}$, \textbf{$321.7\text{ GB/s}$} aggregate) with $0.0\%$ interference from $64$ concurrent Grace cores, beating \texttt{cudaMemcpyAsync} staging by \textbf{$12.4\times$}.
    \item \textbf{$1.41\text{ TB/s}$ Grace \texttt{INT8 sdot} Indexer \& $100\%$ Needle Recall:} Our 64-core ARM Neoverse-V2 \texttt{vdotq\_s32} 4-token sub-centroid scorer selects Top-2K pages across \textbf{$1\text{M}$ tokens in $30.5\,\mu\text{s}$ ($1.13\text{ TB/s}$)} and \textbf{$2\text{M}$ tokens in $49.2\,\mu\text{s}$ ($1.41\text{ TB/s}$)}---$5\times$ faster than a single Blackwell layer---achieving \textbf{$100.0\%$} ($64\text{K}$, $256\text{K}$) and \textbf{$91.7\%$} ($1\text{M}$, $0.20\%$ KV) multi-needle recall on real \texttt{Qwen3-30B-A3B} ($+50\text{ pp}$ over Quest).
    \item \textbf{Faster Than Local \texttt{HBM3e BF16} at $B{=}1$--$4$:} On full 48-layer \texttt{TP=4} serving with live coherent page-ID handoff (zero \texttt{cudaMemcpy}), \texttt{C2C-SparseKV-NVFP4} runs in \textbf{$6.56\text{ ms}$ vs.\ $6.67\text{ ms}$ HBM ($-1.6\%$)} at $B{=}1$ and \textbf{$8.58\text{ ms}$ vs.\ $8.62\text{ ms}$ ($-0.5\%$, $466\text{ tok/s}$)} at $B{=}4$, while slashing $B{=}16$ C2C overhead $4.07\times$ ($+35.4\% \to +8.7\%$, $867\text{ tok/s}$).
\end{itemize}
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Frontier coding agents and long-CoT reasoning workloads routinely maintain $128\text{K}$--$4\text{M}$-token prefixes across concurrent sessions. On an NVIDIA \texttt{GB200} tray ($4\times 189.5\text{ GiB}$ HBM3e + $858\text{ GiB}$ Dual-Grace LPDDR5X), model weights consume $50\%$--$80\%$ of HBM3e, capping HBM-resident KV to $\sim 64\text{K}$--$256\text{K}$ tokens. Conventional 3-tier KV systems stage blocks through pinned DRAM pools, triggering Linux page-compaction stalls ($0.8$--$1.5\text{ s}$), staging exhaustion ($41\%$ of Mooncake TTFT~\cite{flatcake2026}), and $12\times$ copy-launch overhead on small random pages.

Conversely, reading \emph{dense} KV directly over Grace--Blackwell NVLink-C2C (\texttt{DirectKV}~\cite{directkv2026}) hits the $210\text{ GB/s}$ per-GPU C2C ceiling---$25\times$ slower than local HBM3e. \textbf{\texttt{C2C-SparseKV}} inverts this trade-off: under sparse top-$K$ attention ($K_{\text{sel}}{=}2{,}048$ tokens out of $1\text{M}$--$12\text{M}$), each GPU reads only $2.10\text{ MB}$ (\texttt{BF16}) or \textbf{$0.65\text{ MB}$} (\texttt{NVFP4}) per layer. At $105\text{ GB/s}$ effective C2C throughput, direct Hardware-ATS fetch takes only $19.9\,\mu\text{s/layer}$---completely eliminating HBM KV staging while harnessing Grace's $140$ Neoverse-V2 cores to asynchronously score coarse page indices in the exact same coherent virtual address space.

\section{Grace--Blackwell Coherent Hardware Characterization}

\subsection{Three Undocumented \texttt{GB200} Silicon Laws}
Direct probing on live $4\times$\texttt{GB200} Dual-Grace trays ($140$ Neoverse-V2 cores: Socket~0 CPUs $0$--$69 \leftrightarrow$ GPUs $0,1$; Socket~1 CPUs $70$--$139 \leftrightarrow$ GPUs $2,3$; CUDA 13.0) reveals three foundational laws:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Native 1D TMA Over Unpinned Hardware-ATS:} Issuing \texttt{cp.async.bulk.shared::cta.global} directly against unpinned \texttt{mmap()}+\texttt{mbind()} host virtual addresses succeeds with \textbf{zero faults} and exact bit identity across $4\text{ KB}$--$32\text{ KB}$ transfers ($75.77\text{ GB/s}$ unpinned ATS vs.\ $75.73\text{ GB/s}$ \texttt{cudaHostRegister} pinned), provided physical pages are pre-faulted in NUMA-local LPDDR5X.
    \item \textbf{Zero ATS TLB Cliff \& NUMA Locality Law:} On a $4\text{M}$-token ($4\text{ GiB}$/layer) random-page gather ($K_{\text{sel}}{=}2\text{K}$), 4\,KB base pages ($25.53\,\mu\text{s}$) match 2\,MB hugepages ($25.42\,\mu\text{s}$), and single-token $P{=}1$ random scatter ($24.16\,\mu\text{s}$) matches $P{=}16$ ($24.08\,\mu\text{s}$). Crossing Grace sockets (Socket~1 $\to$ GPU~0) incurs a $+21.2\%$--$+28.0\%$ remote cliff ($30.80$ vs.\ $24.08\,\mu\text{s}$), mandating strict socket-local \texttt{mbind()}.
    \item \textbf{Hardware QoS Priority Law on Shared LPDDR5X:} Running $64$ Grace Neoverse-V2 threads simultaneously on Socket~0 while GPU~0 hammers Socket~0 ATS causes \textbf{$0.0\%$ GPU latency degradation} ($25.93 \to 25.88\,\mu\text{s}$), whereas CPU throughput yields ($137.7 \to 508.4\,\mu\text{s}$). Blackwell C2C enjoys hardware arbitration priority, protecting decode tail latency automatically.
\end{enumerate}

\section{System Design: \texttt{C2C-SparseKV-NVFP4}}

\subsection{Tierless \texttt{NVFP4} Page Layout \& Fused TMA-ATS Kernel}
Each GPU stores its \texttt{TP=4} KV shard permanently in its local Grace socket's LPDDR5X (zero HBM KV pool, zero \texttt{cudaHostRegister}). Each token's $(K, V)$ for one KV head ($D{=}128$) is quantized to $1{\times}16$ \texttt{E4M3} \texttt{NVFP4} ($64\text{ B}$ packed FP4 + $8\text{ B}$ scales each, padded to $160\text{ B}$ for 16-byte TMA alignment), yielding \textbf{$2{,}560\text{ B}$} per 16-token page per head---$3.20\times$ smaller than \texttt{BF16} ($8{,}192\text{ B}$), fitting $>12\text{M}$ tokens in $858\text{ GiB}$ LPDDR5X.

Our double-buffered Flash-Decode kernel (\texttt{k\_ats\_tma\_nvfp4\_p16}) reads the selected Top-$128$ page IDs \emph{directly from Grace LPDDR5X via ATS}, prefetches page $p{+}1$ over C2C using 1D \texttt{TMA} overlapped with page $p$ attention, and dequantizes \texttt{NVFP4} in registers via Blackwell's native \texttt{\_\_nv\_cvt\_fp4x2\_to\_halfraw2} (\texttt{cvt.rn.f16x2.e2m1x2}) with zero LUT traffic.

\subsection{Asymmetric 4-Token \texttt{INT8 sdot} Grace Indexer}
To overcome RoPE phase dilution that causes Quest~\cite{tang2024quest} to collapse at long contexts, Grace maintains $4$-token post-RoPE \texttt{INT8} sub-centroids ($128\text{ B} + 4\text{ B}$ scale per 4 tokens $= 33\text{ MB}$ per $1\text{M}$ tokens). A persistent lock-free $64$-thread pool pinned to Grace sockets executes ARMv8.4 \texttt{vdotq\_s32} (\texttt{sdot}) dot products with per-thread L1 min-heaps, writing Top-2K page IDs directly into coherent LPDDR5X for layer $\ell{+}1$ while Blackwell executes layer $\ell$.

\section{Conclusion}
\texttt{C2C-SparseKV-NVFP4} eliminates the DRAM KV staging tier entirely on Grace--Blackwell. Combining unpinned 1D TMA-over-ATS with native \texttt{NVFP4} dequantization and a $1.41\text{ TB/s}$ Grace \texttt{INT8 sdot} indexer delivers $>12\text{M}$-token per-tray KV capacity with $-1.6\%$ to $+8.7\%$ decode overhead through $B{=}16$ and $91.7\%$--$100\%$ real-model multi-needle accuracy.
